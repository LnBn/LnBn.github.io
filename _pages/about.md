I am an applied mathematician and a Postdoctoral Research Assistant in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, working with [Dr Athen Ma](https://sites.google.com/view/athenma/home).

My work combines statistical inference, complex networks and dynamical systems. At Queen Mary I use Bayesian stochastic block models to uncover hidden group structure in ecological networks, and large-scale simulations to study how adaptive food webs respond to perturbations, including rare, high-impact events such as extinction cascades. My PhD at the University of Sydney, with Prof. Eduardo Altmann and Prof. Holger Dullin, was on [transient chaos in open dynamical systems](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/32171).

I also apply these tools to market data in independent projects: [what groups US stocks form beyond their industry labels](/projects/sbm-equity-networks/), [how heavy the tails of asset returns are](/projects/tail-risk-surrogates/), and [a no-arbitrage price floor created overnight by a video-game update](/projects/cs2-tradeup-parity/). The code for each is on [GitHub](https://github.com/LnBn).

**Interests:** Bayesian inference and model selection · complex networks · dynamical systems and chaos · rare events and heavy tails · financial time series.
