I am an applied mathematician and a Postdoctoral Research Assistant in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, working with [Dr Athen Ma](https://sites.google.com/view/athenma/home).

My work combines statistical inference, complex networks and dynamical systems. In my recent work I use Bayesian stochastic block models to uncover hidden group structure in ecological networks, and large-scale simulations to study how adaptive food webs respond to perturbations, including rare, high-impact events such as extinction cascades. My PhD at the University of Sydney was on [transient chaos in open dynamical systems](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/32171).

**Interests:** Dynamical Systems and Chaos · Bayesian Inference · Machine Learning · Complex Systems · Time Series · AI
